Hi, I'm Xingting.
Designer, researcher,
and illustrator.

I'm a PhD candidate at the School of Design and Architecture, Swinburne University of Technology (Melbourne), supervised by Professor Sonja Pedell.

My research focuses on participatory design with older adults in residential aged care — exploring crafting, gardening, and outdoor ecological discovery as pathways to wellbeing and connection.

🌿Studying how older adults connect with nature, craft, and technology in aged care in Melbourne.
🎲Board game enthusiast — especially Splendor Duel.
🤖Curious about speculative design, design fiction, and how AI is reshaping creative identity in art education.
💪Volunteer at an aged care visiting service (ACVVS) in Melbourne.
📮 Sharing academic reading notes and artwork on 小红书 Xiaohongshu.
