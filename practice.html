Physical making

ProtoLAB &
fabrication.

Turning digital drawings into physical pieces that people can assemble, decorate, and make their own.

Skills Vector drawing · Laser cutting · Material prototyping

Design process

  • Draw. Prepare outlines, slots, and bases in Adobe Illustrator.
  • Make. Laser-cut wooden parts at Swinburne ProtoLAB.
  • Refine. Use workshop observations and staff feedback to simplify assembly.
Follow the design iterations

Output

  • Wooden figures to assemble and personalise.
  • Colour, fabric, and wool chosen by residents.
  • A shared Nativity scene in the garden.

Visual communication

Visual &
information design.

Cards to carry, information beside the plants, and a large map to explore together.

Skills Information design · Print production · Spatial illustration

Design process

  • Recognise. Use photographs of the plants along the walking route.
  • Explore. Pair images with short prompts to look, try, and talk.
  • Make. Produce cards for carrying and hanging outdoors.

Card specifications

Size
12 × 18 cm
Material
3 mm acrylic
Printing
UV printing
Formats
Hand-held & hanging

Output in use

Treasure hunt cards

  • Hold a card during the walk.
  • Match its photograph with the plant.
  • Pause to notice a detail or share a thought.

Plant information cards

  • Hang the card beside its plant.
  • Keep the name, photograph, and prompts together.
  • Make the information visible on later walks.

A shared circular map

  • Bring the walking route into one large visual.
  • Point to familiar plants and places together.
  • Revisit outdoor experiences in an indoor conversation.
